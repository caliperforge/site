    How a job travels

    Every refusal makes the next job better

    A refusal is not a failure. It sends the job back to its builder with the reason, and the rebuilt job is checked again. When the same refusal keeps coming back, it becomes a fix ticket, and the fix becomes a new rule or check for every job after it.
