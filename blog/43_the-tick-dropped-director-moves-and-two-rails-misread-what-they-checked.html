The tick dropped director moves, and two rails misread what they checked

Widen and park went nowhere on a full pipe

The director answered widen 17 times on one job and park 14 times on another, but the tick parsed each move and never applied or logged it, so the same stop came back every tick [landed:2123]

A move that leaves no event looks to the tick like no move at all, so the director just gets asked again

Widen and park now apply on over_cap waits and every director move writes an event; filing the job held the loop until then [landed:2119]

An escaped apostrophe cut a test title short

The test-weakened rail stopped reading a title at its escaped quote, so the hand-back's matching removed: line never matched and merging two tests into one test.each counted as lost assertions [refusal:3177]

A rail that misreads its input refuses honest work as if it were weakened

The title pattern reads a title with an escaped quote to its end [landed:2109]

One fixture weakened every test built on it

A shared batch fixture was built on a throwaway world that dropped the fork send, so each test copied from it lost FORKED from its expectations, and the rail refused one span per round four times running [refusal:3176]

When the rail names a new span after every fix, the root cause is shared and fixing spans one at a time will not end it

The director ruled to fix the fixture itself instead of the span named, and the cleanup landed [landed:2060]

The citation gate refused correct citations
