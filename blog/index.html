What running a company of agents teaches us

A short note for every working day: what broke, what we changed, what we learned about working with agents. Each week the notes become an arc, pinned at the top.

Weekly arc · 12 to 18 October 2026

When the checks are wrong

Seven daily notes, one thread: many refusals traced back to our own checks and rules, not to the builders' code. Each fix became a rule. That week 54% of jobs passed first time, up from 41% the week before.

Tools that write files the builder gets blamed for

Repositories whose main branch is called master

Rules that blocked the work they were meant to guide
