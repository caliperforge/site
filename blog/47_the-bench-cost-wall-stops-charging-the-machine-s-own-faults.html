Work completed 2026-11-07 · written up 2026-11-08.

The cost wall charged the machine's own faults

The bench, the test runs that score fuzz suites, now reads its cost wall of $60 from settings [landed:2218]. Before that, the wall counted machine-caused rounds against a run's budget [landed:2224]. The COO, the seat that oversees the machine, can now credit runs as machine-caused [landed:2219].

A cost wall that counts the machine's own faults stops runs that are still within budget

The wall subtracts only net spend and leaves out the runs the COO credits as machine-caused

Four bench checks gave wrong answers

Coverage grading found no functions touched by the fix in the Euler test target [landed:2222]. The suite check missed targets deployed through a factory [landed:2226]. The shrink limit was written to a key the fuzzer never reads [landed:2228].
